Videos
------

I don't like making videos and I'm not good at it, but I realized they
can be effective at teaching, so I've done it.  Most of mine
intentionally do not have my name attached to them, but you can find
some below.

* `HPC-kitchen, series teaching HPC via a kitchen metaphor
  <https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZLVmS9rf3nNDHRo1Baz_JVQWDI0mTYyB>`__
  (planned, performed, and produced by me with various advice from
  CodeRefinery)
* Teaching tech vs teaching games aka `Motivation to CodeRefinery
  train-the-trainer <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61Cdi4Eje2Y>`__.
* I developed the `CodeRefinery livestream teaching strategy
  <https://coderefinery.github.io/manuals/instructor-stream/>`__
* I developed `ffmpeg-editlist
  <https://github.com/coderefinery/ffmpeg-editlist>`__ to edit videos
  via YAML files.
